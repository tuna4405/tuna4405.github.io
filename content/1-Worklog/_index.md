This worklog covers the internship from 01/06/2026 onward, recorded weekly.

The first six weeks were spent studying the AWS services and the underlying
computing concepts needed to design and build a real system. The final three
weeks were spent building that system: **Caerus**, a cinema seat booking
platform, developed by a two-person team and deployed across compute, storage,
database, serverless, and monitoring services.

carried out day by day, and what was actually achieved.
